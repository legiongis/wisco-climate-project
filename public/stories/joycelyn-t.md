> Let your voice be heard; Speak up!

An unknown WE Energies issue came about for me back in early 2012. Upon my time limited stay at a local shelter, I was in search of housing which I did acquire but one of their requirements for possible approval was that I establish utilities in my name, what sounds like no-brainer, right(?)

Well, little did I know and very well soon found out I had been a victim of identity theft because there was already a WE Energies account with my name attached to it. Mind you all my previous residency came with utilities included, so, yes I was none-the-wiser of knowing someone, somewhere had used my name to get utilities.

After finding this out, WE Energies readily denied me services and rushed to end the conversation, but knowing what I know I respectfully interrupted the customer service representative and explained my current situation. I asked for all necessary information and/or resources to possibly resolve this issue as soon as possible.

Reluctantly, the representative gave me all the necessary information along with a thorough explanation on how this has happened to others in the past.

All I know is that when it comes to individuals I would advise that we as people not let large companies and/or corporations push us around or brush us off. No your rights and by all means never hesitate to ask all questions necessary.
