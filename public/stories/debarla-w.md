> I'm here to support and stand up fights in the energy burden. This is a subject that affects us all at hand, one way or another, especially the black and brown people here in Milwaukee.

53210 area code and I'm here to support and stand up fights in the energy burden. This is a subject that affects us all at hand, one way or another, especially the black and brown people here in Milwaukee. To make a long story short, as one myself, along with my family, our family's long battle of history, of not always being able to choose between having food, phone, rent. Children's clothing and shoes. The bare necessities.

This 11% increase on our gas and electricity is outraged and we are pissed. The saying is if we don't stand for something, we'll fall for anything. Question number one, We Energies: How do you expect us to keep up as minorities? The ones who are barely making our other bills meet. That's getting paid minimum wage. The cost of everything is on the rise: Food, shelter, phone bills, Wi-Fi, tuition for our decent schooling and for our children, clothing, household goods, cleaning supplies, and and then this goes on and on.

While I may look healthy to you, I have a medical history. When summer time comes, I need my central air. And when winter time is here, I need my heat. I need my electricity for special machines to help me breathe.

We deserve to live comfortably as hard as we work or have worked here in this city, I even remember a time when my family was affected by a shut off about five years back and it was a miserable time for our family. Despair and boredom, and life threatening issues were at hand. So I said all that to say this: We Energies please. Please stop causing more burden with this increase of 11% on the bills. We already can't keep up.
